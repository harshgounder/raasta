## synthesis

The verified landscape separates into presentation frameworks, research engines, model assets, and deterministic infrastructure. Those categories solve different problems; combining them is more credible than declaring any one repository a complete match.

| Strategy | Mechanism | Scope | Main trade-off | Evidence base | Event-time recommendation |

**Takeaway:** the non-obvious tension is that a product should sometimes refuse while the execution benchmark may still demand a query. Resolve that tension with two explicit views of quality: benchmark execution results for the engine, and evidence/clarification/refusal behavior for the website. Do not silently optimize one and advertise the other.

The most defensible product promise is: **"Ask a question. See the answer, the records, and the assumptions. If the data cannot establish it, the product says why."** That promise is achievable as a bounded composition of existing parts. It does not require claiming a new model, universal data cleaning, guaranteed semantic correctness, or a score this team has not measured.

**Final selection:** Vanna presentation + a locally served, license-cleared <=8B artifact + constrained SQL execution + SQLGlot-assisted inspection + duplicate/value/missing-input checks + Datasette-style evidence drilldown. The small-model artifact is the principal unresolved dependency; the dramatic product angle is not.
