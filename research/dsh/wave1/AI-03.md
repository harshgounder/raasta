## Mind-boggling demo angles (3)
- Upload a short PDF in the browser UI, ask a question that spans multiple sections, and animate the retrieved entities and relations beside the grounded answer. A custom split-screen panel can highlight the source passages and graph path as the user switches between LightRAG’s local, global, and mixed retrieval modes.
- Run a bridge-question challenge over 2WikiMultihopQA: the browser shows the question, supporting paragraphs, and the dataset’s subject–relation–object evidence triples as a path, then reveals which supporting facts ground the answer.
- Show a curated micro-corpus in a browser using MiniLM’s small ONNX embedding weights and, optionally, Qwen3’s larger WebGPU-capable ONNX weights for generation. Preload the graph for this demo and make each answer reveal its connected evidence path; disclose the large model download and that the graph/backend is not thereby made browser-only.

## Risks (3)
- Performance and download size: the cited Qwen3 q4 ONNX file is 919 MB; browser WebGPU support is experimental in many browsers, so a lightweight embedding model or precomputed answers/graphs may be needed for a smooth demo.
- Infrastructure complexity: the graph experience is not established as an end-to-end browser-only app; Neo4j’s backend and frontend may need separate deployment, and RAGFlow recommends a starting configuration of 4 CPU cores, 16 GB RAM, and 50 GB disk.
- Licensing needs review: HotpotQA is CC BY-SA 4.0, 2WikiMultihopQA is listed as Apache-2.0, and the Qwen ONNX weight license was not established in the examined model evidence. Check weight, dataset, and redistribution terms before publishing a fork.

## Missing pieces (3)
- A unified split-screen interface that synchronizes each answer with the exact graph path and supporting source passages; the examined projects offer pieces such as graph exploration, source metadata, or traceable citations, but the requested combined interaction was not established.
- A turnkey browser-only pipeline for PDF ingestion, topic/triple extraction, graph storage, retrieval, and answer generation. The examined graph projects use server-side components; browser-capable inference weights alone do not supply this complete pipeline.
- A small, reproducible demo bundle that connects a curated dataset’s evidence annotations to a prebuilt graph and provides deterministic, judge-friendly example queries is not supplied as one integrated artifact by the examined projects.
