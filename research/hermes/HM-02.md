## Notes for wave 2

- The single strongest lead is Parchi. It is Indian, it already names the exact four public data sources (ICMR AMRSN, CDSCO banned FDCs, NPPA, Jan Aushadhi), and it prices a drug in days of household income. That last idea is the "wow" a non technical judge remembers.
- Nobody found combines DDInter severity data with the Jan Aushadhi price floor and the Indian brand map in one screen. That combination is the obvious build.
- ICMR AMRSN is published as an annual PDF only. If the build needs resistance data, budget time for manual extraction. That is also a moat, because a PDF is why nobody else has it in an app.
- CDSCO has no open API. Any claim of "we check every banned combination" needs the PDF extraction done and shown, or the claim will fall apart under a judge question.
