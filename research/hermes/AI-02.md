## Notes for the build team

- The 8B cap is not a handicap on BIRD. Arctic-Text2SQL-R1-7B reports 68.5% test, OmniSQL-7B is competitive, and the BIRD leaderboard lists several small entries: xorazm-text2sql-0.8b at 59.59 test, Struct-SQL 4B at 60.42, Align-SQL-3B at 66.41, Qwen3.5-2B plus StructCoT at 68.47 dev. All fetched 2026-10-24 from https://bird-bench.github.io/.
- The messy database story is the differentiator. Beaver (MIT) shows a plain LLM at 0% and a hinted one near 30% on real warehouses, and Spider 2.0 shows DIN-SQL at 0.00. Quoting both is more convincing than quoting BIRD alone.
- Cheapest credible stack: OmniSQL-7B or Arctic-R1-7B for SQL, XiYan M-Schema for schema serialisation, a Beaver style clarification step, and a read only sandbox with row limits. That maps directly onto the five BIRD-CRITIC failure modes.
- Live things a judge can click: SuperSonic playground (http://117.72.46.148:9080), XiYan GBI on Alibaba Bailian, SQLBot one Docker command.
- Watch licences: Chat2DB 5.3.0 and later is source available, not OSI; SQLBot is GPLv3 with extra restrictions; Vanna is archived; OmniSQL repo has no licence file even though the dataset is Apache 2.0. XiYan, Dataherald, WrenAI core, DB-GPT and Natural-SQL-7B are clean for a demo.
