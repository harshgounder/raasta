## Notes for wave 2

- There is no single project that does "provenance for a molecule property prediction" end to end. The pieces exist (ELN for the wet lab, Galaxy for the workflow, ORD for the reaction, Chemprop for the model, the Princeton checklist for the audit) but nobody has stitched them into one lab notebook style artefact a non chemist can read. That gap is the HM-01 opportunity.
- The Kaggle polymer competition is the best public story to borrow: a leaderboard, a post-competition report, and a reproducible 3rd place repo.
- ChemOS is unmaintained since 2022. EOS is alive. If the build needs an orchestrator, EOS is the safer base.
