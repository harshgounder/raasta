## Notes for wave 2

- ETHGlobal Tokyo 2026 alone had four agent payment projects, three from the same Intercepta risk service. That means the "check the recipient before paying" idea is already crowded and the differentiator is the after-the-fact revoke (Izanagi) or the approved plan (PlanBound).
- Nobody in this list shows a plain language receipt a non technical judge can read at a glance. That is an open presentation gap, not a tech gap.
- The dev.to article cites NPCI, SEBI and MAS saying agents should not independently authorise payments (GFF 2026, 25 Sept 2026). That is a very strong quote for an Indian hackathon pitch and needs the primary source checked in wave 2.
