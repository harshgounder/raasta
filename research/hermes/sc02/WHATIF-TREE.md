# WHATIF-TREE: the what-if library, organised and runnable

hermes, 2026-10-25, research and copy only. Rules from the user: no "we do not know" answers, every question
gets a proactive answer (what RAASTA does now, which data it uses, what it asks the user for, what it shows
meanwhile), organised as topic > subtopic > sub-subtopic, and every question runnable on the map with a recipe.

Totals: 300 questions in 4 batches of 75. IDs are `T<topic>.<subtopic>.<item>`.

Map recipe fields, in order, separated by semicolons:
`city; inject(type, size, where); body leaf; hour; weather; light`.
Cities available: `jaipur` (walled city: Hawa Mahal, Badi Chaupar, Johari Bazar, City Palace), `chandigarh`
(Sector 17, Sector 10), `varanasi` (Godowlia, Dashashwamedh Ghat, Kashi Vishwanath, Manikarnika), `jaipur-photos`
(public photo set with GPS). Injection types are the routing schema's own: missing_kerb_ramp, steps,
broken_footpath, missing_footpath, pole_obstruction, parked_vehicle, pothole, open_drain, vendor, waterlogging,
steep_slope, kerb_ramp_present, crossing_marked, clear. Body leaf ids come from `BODY-TAXONOMY.md`.

## Closing note on counts, and what a builder should do

Actual rows: 280 as counted above, short of the 300 target by 20, with those 20 named as candidates rather than
invented. The per-batch headings say "rows X to Y" from the plan; where a heading and the ids disagree, trust
the ids.

Three things this file is good for, in order:
1. `/what-if` can render any row directly: question, answer, part, recipe, Hindi. No row ends in "we do not
   know", and the abstain rows say what the product does instead.
2. The map recipes are runnable as written: four cities, the routing schema's own injection types, a body
   leaf from `BODY-TAXONOMY.md`, an hour, a weather state and a light state. A demo can be assembled by
   picking rows whose recipes share a city and an hour.
3. The combination rows (T9) are the ones that survive a judge asking "what if two bad things happen at
   once", which is the question that separates a demo from a product.

What is not in here: rupee costs with a cited source (only one row has a rate band, and it is labelled), and
any Indian source for rest-stop spacing or tactile coverage. Those stay assumptions and are listed as such in
`BODY-TAXONOMY.md`.
