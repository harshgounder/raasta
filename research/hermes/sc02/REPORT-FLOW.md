# REPORT-FLOW: the report form, the status trail, and who receives what

hermes, 2026-10-25 00:35 IST, research and copy only. Three parts: the fields a person actually needs to
describe their problem, the honest status trail with real times and triggers, and the authority each barrier
goes to in Jaipur with the sources I could open tonight.

Rule running through all of it: the person reporting is doing the city a favour. Every field is optional
except the photo and the spot, and no question asks them to justify their body or their right to the path.

## Part 1, the form as a branching question tree

### Step 0, shared by every category (4 questions, about 20 seconds)

| # | field, plain words | Hindi | why it is asked | required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1 | What is in your way? (pick one picture) | रास्ते में क्या है? (तस्वीर चुनें) | routes the branch and the detector class | yes |
| 0.2 | Where exactly? (auto from GPS, or tap the map, or "as I walk past it") | ठीक कहाँ? (GPS, नक्शा, या चलते हुए) | attaches to a segment; a 60 m GPS error is refused, not guessed | yes |
| 0.3 | One photo, from where you are standing | एक फ़ोटो, जहाँ खड़े हैं वहीं से | the only evidence that survives scrutiny | yes |

### Step 9, closing questions (every branch, 3 questions)

| # | field | Hindi | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | Can we contact you if the city asks a question? | शहर वाले पूछें तो हम संपर्क कर सकते हैं? | phone or none; default is anonymous |
| 9.2 | Do you want your name on the complaint? | शिकायत में नाम दें? | yes / no, keep me anonymous |
| 9.3 | Should this also warn other people right now, or only the city? | दूसरों को भी चेतावनी दें या सिर्फ़ नगर निगम को? | warn everyone / city only / both |

Design rules baked into the tree: no branch exceeds five questions, every question has "not sure", the photo
and the spot are the only hard requirements, and the free-text box accepts any language because a person
describing a blocked footpath should not have to write in English.

## Part 2, the honest status trail

The wording below is what the app should print, in order, with the time and the trigger for each step. The
times are the ones our measured pipeline supports; where a step depends on a human, the range is wider and
the copy says so.

| # | state the person sees | Hindi | when it happens | trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Received. Your photo is saved with the time and the spot.** | मिल गया। फ़ोटो समय और जगह के साथ सुरक्षित है। | instantly on upload | upload completes, hash stored |
| 2 | **First look by the system: about 5 seconds.** | सिस्टम ने देख लिया: लगभग 5 सेकंड। | within 10 s | detectors run, median 4.26 s per photo measured on this box |
| 3 | **What the system could see: a blocked ramp, 23 cm, on the north side.** | सिस्टम को दिखा: रैंप बंद, 23 सेमी, उत्तर ओर। | same message as step 2 | at least two detectors agree, else the copy says "not clear enough to measure" |
| 4 | **Your route is updated for you now.** | आपके लिए रास्ता अभी बदल गया। | within 30 s | barrier attached at p >= 0.7 and route recomputed |
| 5 | **On the map for everyone in the next update, usually within an hour.** | सबके नक्शे पर अगले अपडेट में, आमतौर पर एक घंटे में। | up to 60 min | batch publish, because a public map should not flip on one unreviewed photo |
| 6 | **A person will check it within 24 hours.** | कोई इंसान 24 घंटे में जाँचेगा। | queued at step 5 | human review queue; the copy admits the person exists |
| 7 | **Sent to the ward office on the date shown. Reference number inside.** | तारीख़ के साथ वार्ड कार्यालय भेजा गया। संदर्भ संख्या अंदर है। | within 24 hours of human check | complaint generated, authority resolved |
| 8 | **No answer in 30 days? You can escalate with one tap, with the same evidence.** | 30 दिन में जवाब नहीं? उसी सबूत के साथ एक टैप में आगे भेजें। | offered on day 30 | statutory window passes; escalation draft prepared (state disability commissioner, or the municipal grievance route) |

Two honesty rules in the copy.

1. Step 3 never claims more than the detectors support. If the photo is night-blurred, the message says "we
   could not measure this, it is on the map as unknown", and step 4 still happens with the unknown penalty,
   because the person's route should still get safer.
2. Step 5 is deliberately slower than step 4. The person who reported gets the benefit immediately, and the
   public map waits for a batch, which is the difference between a trusted ledger and a feed anyone can
   poison.

What removes a report: a later photo of the same spot showing the fix, or a verified repair from the works
record. What never removes it: the authority marking a ticket closed. We keep both facts and show the
difference, which is the single most useful thing the ledger does for the person who reported.

## Part 3, which authority receives which barrier in Jaipur

| barrier type | authority to address | the copy line on the complaint | source status |
|---|---|---|---|
| missing or broken kerb ramp, broken footpath, pavers, footpath obstruction by shop or material | **JMC Greater or JMC Heritage ward office** (which one depends on the ward inside the old city boundary) | "Ward office, footpath repair under the accessibility duty" | Municipal Corporation Jaipur site and the Rajasthan municipal grievance route opened tonight: jaipurmc.org, rajmunicipal.com toll free 18005728545 |
| parked vehicles on the footpath, including valet rows and showroom parking | **Jaipur Traffic Police**, with the ward copied | "Parking enforcement on a footpath, photo and time attached" | Jaipur Traffic Police app exists and the state has published a stricter parking-challan plan; both seen at headline level, not read in full |
| footpath on a state highway or a major arterial maintained by the state | **Rajasthan PWD**, with the ward copied | "State road footpath, kerb and ramp defect" | Named as the structural owner of state roads; the specific Jaipur division office was not verified tonight |
| metro corridor, station approach, footpath taken for metro works | **Jaipur Metro Rail Corporation**, with the ward copied | "Metro works corridor, temporary footpath diversion not maintained" | Named; project-board location is the practical address, the office contact was not verified |
| railway land, level crossings, footpaths along the tracks | **Divisional Railway Manager, Jaipur**, with the ward copied | "Railway land footpath or crossing plate defect" | Named; not verified tonight |
| private colony internal roads | **Residents' welfare association**, with the ward informed | "Private colony footpath, RWA responsible, ward informed" | Structural, not verified |
| open drain, manhole or sewage overflow | **JMC public health or drainage wing**, with the ward | "Open drain or manhole, safety class, handle within 24 hours" | Named, mirrors the JMC structure; not verified |
| street lighting on the footpath | **JMC electricity wing**, or the discom where the pole belongs | "Footpath lighting, unlit stretch" | Named; discom split not verified |
| construction site blocking the footpath | **the site's approving authority**, usually the JMC building department, with the contractor named | "Site blocking the footpath, barricade or material outside the approved boundary" | Named, based on the construction-tag approach in the routing data |
| anything unresolved after 30 days | **State Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities, Rajasthan**, with the municipal grievance route | "Accessibility duty not discharged, evidence attached, escalation" | Institution exists under the RPwD Act; the specific office address was not verified tonight |

The honest caveat for the deck: only the municipal corporation line is source-backed tonight (jaipurmc.org and
the rajmunicipal grievance page with its toll free number). The rest are structurally correct owners, named so
the complaint has somewhere to go, and each needs one verification call before the site prints an address.

## What the page should never do

1. Never say "your complaint has been received by the city" before the complaint is actually generated and
   the reference exists. Ours says "received by us", which is true the second the upload lands.
2. Never promise a repair date. The city owns that clock, and a promise we do not control is the fastest way
   to lose a user's trust.
3. Never ask for a disability certificate to accept an accessibility report. The path is public, and so is
   the right to report it.
