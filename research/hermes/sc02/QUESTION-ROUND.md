# QUESTION ROUND, SC-02

For every element that could exist on an Indian footpath route: the question a judge or user would ask, the honest answer the system can give, and the product part that handles it.

Source of elements: `research/claude/sc02-FACTOR-UNIVERSE.md` (348 factors, 14 layers, 165 what-ifs), plus 21 elements missing from it. Answers never exceed what the observed-via field supports; `UNKNOWN` and `abstain` are treated as first-class outcomes, not failures.

Parts: scan (detect in imagery), map (graph and segments), route cost (profile penalties), receipt (evidence shown to the user or city), abstain (refuse to answer), unknown (missing evidence), complaint (user or city action).
