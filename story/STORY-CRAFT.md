## 1.5 The pity trap: Stella Young (must read)

- Term: "inspiration porn. And I use the term porn deliberately, because they objectify one group of people for the benefit of another group of people. So in this case, we're objectifying disabled people for the benefit of nondisabled people." (TEDxSydney, April 2014)
- "We've been sold the lie that disability is a Bad Thing ... and to live with a disability makes you exceptional. It's not a bad thing, and it doesn't make you exceptional."
- "The things that we're overcoming are not the things that you think they are. They are not things to do with our bodies ... the social model of disability, which tells us that we are more disabled by the society that we live in than by our bodies and our diagnoses."
- "No amount of smiling at a flight of stairs has ever made it turn into a ramp. Never."

Sources: https://www.ted.com/talks/stella_young_i_m_not_your_inspiration_thank_you_very_much (2014-06-09); https://singjupost.com/im-not-your-inspiration-thank-you-very-much-stella-young-transcript (2020-04-18); https://blog.ted.com/remembering-stella-young (2014-12-07); https://www.abc.net.au/news/2014-12-08/17-things-stella-young-wanted-you-to-know/5950814 (2014-12-08); https://speakola.com/ideas/stella-young-inspiration-porn-tedx-2014 (2016-01-19).

## 6. Reasoning log (whys and mistakes)

- Why 7 beats and not 6: the spine has 8 sentences; "until finally" and "ever since then" share B7, which leaves 7. Six beats would have merged the receipt and the route, and the user wants the chain photo -> receipt -> route visible as separate steps.
- Why the happy end is a route and not a ramp: the user said the end is happy BECAUSE they use our product. A ramp built overnight is the city's act, not the product's, and it would be a fake promise. So ART-BRIEF #12 needs one change (no new ramp; Billu on the same kerb). Flag this to codex before it renders #12.
- Why Zoya says "Shoot low. Show the edge." in B5: without it, B5 makes Kabir the hero (he takes the photo, the app answers). Loop and Stella Young say the disabled character leads; Zoya knows kerbs better than anyone, so she directs the shot.
- Why "Ever since, Kabir looks down": Float and The Present both resolve on the person who changed; Adams' 8th sentence is where change becomes habit. It also pays off B2 ("sees only his screen") with Before Your Eyes logic from STORY-RESEARCH T15 (the device that blinded him now measures).
- Why I dropped the push meter beat from STORY-RESEARCH 2.5: it shows Zoya straining at a kerb. Stella Young's test says effort is not the story; showing the strain risks pity. The 5 pm blockage carries the problem without her suffering.
- Why I dropped Rekha (JE) and the complaint from the story: Coats #5 "combine characters, hop over detours"; 145 s cannot hold a seventh human. The complaint stays in the demo after B7.
- Mistake caught: my first draft had "Billu sleeps on the new ramp" (copied from STORY-v1 beat 6). That ramp contradicts "happy because of the product". Replaced with Billu jumping the same kerb.
- Mistake caught: I almost listed Cuerdas' ending as fact; it was memory, so it is UNVERIFIED above.
- Measured, not eyeballed: the longest English on-screen line is 12 words (the receipt), checked by script; beat times sum to 145 s; 0 em or en dashes; 0 banned words.
- Gap: no Indian disability short with both an award and a director interview was found in 31 calls. Chuskit (feature) and AbilityFEST (format) are the closest sourced Indian anchors.
